| D-29 | **Patient auth reverts to staff-issued passwords** (2026-08-29): no self-registration, no OTP of any kind (email, SMS or WhatsApp). A patient contacts the clinic's WhatsApp Business number — a human-staffed channel, not a technical integration; staff verify who they are (trained, not automated) and create the account on their behalf via `POST /patients`, setting a permanent, machine-generated password the patient does not choose. Forgetting it is the same WhatsApp conversation again (`POST /patients/{id}/reset-password`), never a self-service reset. Full detail: [docs/runbooks/patient-account-provisioning.md](../runbooks/patient-account-provisioning.md) |
| D-30 | **Clinician onboarding reverts to staff-issued credentials** (2026-08-29): no Cognito invite email, ever. The principal clinician creates a colleague's account via `POST /clinicians` exactly as today, but this platform now generates a permanent password *and* completes the clinician pool's mandatory TOTP enrolment on the new clinician's behalf, returning both the password and the TOTP setup secret in the same API response, once. The principal relays both to the new clinician however they already communicate — in person, phone, WhatsApp — never automatically emailed, never a Cognito-sent temporary-password email. Deactivation/reactivation and the principal/sub role split are unchanged. Supersedes 2.4.1's own "Cognito's own invite email, a deliberate deviation" note (`clinician-admin.ts`'s header) and closes the clinician half of D-09 the same way D-29 already closed the patient half | Removes the last email dependency from account creation of any kind — clinician onboarding no longer needs SES production access (LL-01) any more than patient onboarding does after D-29, and a stalled SES appeal no longer blocks hiring a colleague. Mitigated by the audit trail (every create is attributed to the acting principal) and, not built in this pass, a real mitigation named rather than silently deferred: a signed-in clinician replacing their own admin-provisioned TOTP secret with one only they have ever seen.
